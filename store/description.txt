Time first. Everything else only when it matters.

Hush is an adaptive quiet watchface for Pebble Time 2 and Round 2. Most of the day you see large readable time, one optional soft detail, and a calm empty rail. When something crosses a threshold you set—calendar, rain, step pace, battery, sunrise, or a second-timezone overlap—a colored pip lights up. Flick your wrist for a five-second detail card, then the face returns to quiet.

Customize themes (Ink, Paper, Night, Phosphor, Coral, or your own colors), always-on slot, and every signal threshold. One-tap presets for Commuter, Parent, Deep Work, and Weekend. Battery-friendly by design: minute updates, cached weather and calendar, no live heart-rate sampling, and a low-power mode at 15%.
